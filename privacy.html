Privacy notice

Last updated 18 October 2026

This notice explains how Reading Hockey Club ("the club", "we") uses personal information in its online claims system, which is used to submit, approve and pay expenses, invoices, payroll hours and mileage.

Who is responsible for your information

The club is the data controller. For any question about your information, or to use your rights below, contact the club secretary.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, club role, and when you accepted this notice and the terms of use.
  • Claim details: dates, descriptions, budget area and team, hours worked, hourly rates, journeys and miles, the number of colleagues you drove, amounts, invoice numbers and pay periods.
  • Bank details: the name, sort code and account number you want to be paid into, and when they were last changed. For supplier invoices, the supplier's bank details as shown on their invoice.
  • Uploads: receipts and invoices you attach. These can contain other details, such as a supplier or your own business details.
  • Approval records: who approved, returned or paid each claim, and when.
  • Technical information: sign-in records and basic logs kept by our service providers for security, such as your IP address and the time of a request.

Please don't upload documents containing information we don't need, such as medical details or other people's personal information. Cover it up before uploading if you can.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To process and pay your claims. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you, whether you're paid through payroll, invoice the club, or are being reimbursed.
  • To keep financial and tax records. This is a legal obligation, for example HMRC and PAYE record-keeping.
  • To pay you into the bank account you give us. This is necessary to perform our agreement with you.
  • To run the club's finances properly. This covers approving spending, budget tracking, preventing fraud or mistakes, and keeping the system secure. These are our legitimate interests, and we've balanced them against your rights.
  • To email you about your account and claims, for example when a claim is approved, returned or paid. This is part of running the service you've signed up to.

We don't use your information for marketing, and we don't make decisions about you by automated means.

Who can see it

  • You: your own account and claims.
  • Approvers: the volunteers the club has chosen to approve a type of claim see claims of that type, with their uploads.
  • The bookkeeper and club admins: all claims, for payment, accounting and running the system.
  • Bank details are visible only to you and the bookkeeper, not to approvers or other admins. When they change, you and the bookkeeper are emailed, to protect against fraud.
  • Our accountants, auditors or HMRC, where needed for accounting, audit or legal reasons.

We use these service providers, who process information only on our instructions:

  • Supabase for the database, sign-in and file storage. Our project is hosted in the London region.
  • Resend to send system emails.
  • GitHub Pages to host the website. It may log basic connection details such as IP addresses.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where information is transferred outside the UK, they protect it with approved safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

  • Claims, uploads and approval records: six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required for financial and tax records. They are then deleted.
  • Bank details: while your account is active. They're removed when your account is closed, except where they form part of the record of a payment already made.
  • Your account: while you're involved with the club. If you leave, we deactivate your account. Your account details are kept only as long as needed to identify the claims above.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your information (you can download your account and claims from My account → Download my data)
  • have inaccurate information corrected
  • ask us to delete your information, or restrict how we use it
  • object to how we use it
  • receive your information in a portable format

Some rights are limited where we must keep financial records by law. We'll explain if that applies. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and browser storage

The system doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in. It also keeps an unsent claim draft on your device so you don't lose your work. These are strictly necessary for the service, so they don't need consent. Signing out removes the sign-in token.

Security

Access is controlled by personal accounts and passwords, and by rules in the database that limit what each role can see. Uploads are stored privately and opened only through short-lived links. Keep your password private, and tell the club straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. If we make important changes, we'll tell you when you next sign in.
