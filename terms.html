Terms of use

Last updated 18 October 2026

These terms apply to everyone who uses the Reading Hockey Club claims system. By creating an account you agree to them. They sit alongside the club's expenses, coaching and officiating policies. If anything here conflicts with those policies, the club's policies apply.

1. Who can use the system

The system is for club coaches, umpires, volunteers and officers who are entitled to claim from the club, and for the people who approve and pay those claims. The club activates each account and decides each person's role. The club can suspend or close an account at any time, for example when someone leaves the club.

2. Your account

  • Use your own account only. Don't share your password or let anyone submit claims for you.
  • Keep your name and email address up to date in My account.
  • Tell the club straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

3. Making claims

  • Only claim for costs or work that were for club business, that you actually incurred or carried out, and that you haven't claimed before or been paid for elsewhere.
  • Make sure dates, hours, journeys, miles and amounts are accurate. Attach a genuine receipt or invoice where the form asks for one.
  • Hourly rates and the mileage rate, including any free miles and the extra for carrying colleagues, are set by the club and applied automatically.
  • Submit claims promptly, in line with any deadlines the club sets, for example by the end of each month or before the financial year closes.
  • Keep your bank details in My account correct. The club isn't responsible for a payment made to the account you gave. The club will never ask you for bank details by email or text. If you get a message asking you to change them, don't reply. Tell the club.
  • When logging a supplier invoice, copy the bank details from the invoice. If a supplier says their bank details have changed, confirm it by phoning them on a number you already know before submitting.
  • If you're self-employed and invoice the club, you're responsible for your own tax and National Insurance.

4. Approval and payment

  • Each claim needs approval from two different authorised approvers, after which it passes to the bookkeeper for payment.
  • Approvers can return a claim with a reason. You can then correct and resubmit it, which restarts the approvals.
  • Approval isn't a guarantee of payment if a claim later turns out to be wrong. The club can correct mistakes, including by recovering an overpayment.
  • Payroll claims are paid through the club's payroll, with the usual deductions. Other claims are paid in the way the bookkeeper arranges.

5. Approvers, bookkeepers and admins

If you have one of these roles, you'll see other people's claims and personal information. Use it only for approving, paying and administering claims. Keep it confidential, and follow the club's privacy notice. Don't approve anything you have a personal interest in. Ask another approver instead.

6. Misuse

Submitting a false, inflated or duplicate claim, or trying to get around the system's controls, may lead to the claim being refused, money being recovered, action under the club's disciplinary procedures, and, where appropriate, a report to the police.

7. Availability

We aim to keep the system available, but we can't guarantee it will always work without interruption. If you can't submit a claim on time because the system is unavailable, contact the club. Keep your own copies of receipts and invoices until your claim has been paid.

8. Your information

How we use your personal information is explained in the privacy notice.

9. Changes

The club may update these terms. If we make important changes, we'll tell you when you next sign in. If you keep using the system after that, you're accepting the updated terms.

10. Contact and law

Questions about these terms: the club secretary. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
